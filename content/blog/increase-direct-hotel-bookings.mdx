Online travel agencies can introduce a hotel to new audiences, while a property's own website provides another way for travellers to discover it and make a reservation.

Building a stronger direct booking channel is not just a matter of launching a website. The experience needs to give potential guests clear information and a straightforward path from interest to enquiry or booking.

## Make the essentials easy to find

Visitors should quickly understand what makes your property suitable for their trip.

Present room types, amenities, location, photographs, policies, and contact information clearly. Keep important details easy to find on both desktop and mobile devices.

If guests have to search through several pages to answer basic questions, they may leave before making an enquiry.

## Give people a reason to choose your property

A direct channel should communicate the experience, not just list facilities.

Show what makes the property distinctive. This might include its location, room design, dining options, service, nearby attractions, or suitability for a particular type of traveller.

Use accurate photography and specific descriptions. Clear information helps guests decide whether the property fits their needs.

## Reduce friction in the booking journey

Review the path from the first page visit to the final booking step.

Are room options easy to understand? Can visitors check availability without unnecessary steps? Are policies and additional charges explained before a guest commits?

If you use an enquiry form or booking integration, make sure the next action is obvious and that enquiries reach the right team.

## Help the right people discover your website

A website needs visibility as well as a good user experience.

Search engine optimisation can help travellers find relevant property pages. Useful local information, well-written room descriptions, and accurate business details can support discovery.

Paid campaigns on Google or Meta may also be useful when their audiences, targeting, and costs align with your commercial goals. Track enquiries and completed bookings wherever practical rather than judging campaigns by clicks alone.

## Measure what happens after the visit

Website traffic is only one part of the picture.

Review enquiry volume, booking conversion, mobile performance, the pages visitors use, and where traffic originates. Where tracking permits, compare the cost of acquiring direct bookings with the costs associated with other channels.

Use these findings to prioritise improvements instead of redesigning the website without a clear objective.

## The takeaway

A stronger direct booking channel combines a clear website, useful property information, a straightforward booking journey, and marketing that reaches relevant travellers.

Direct bookings do not replace every other distribution channel. They give hotels another way to connect with guests and develop a more balanced approach to distribution.
