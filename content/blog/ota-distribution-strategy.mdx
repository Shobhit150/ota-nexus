Online travel agencies can help hotels reach travellers who might never discover a property through its own website. But simply adding more platforms does not automatically lead to more profitable bookings.

A useful distribution strategy starts with a clearer question: which channels are bringing the right guests to your property?

## Start with the channels that matter

Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, and other booking platforms serve different audiences and markets. Their value depends on your location, property type, guest profile, and commercial goals.

Instead of treating every channel equally, review where your bookings originate, which room types perform well, and which markets you want to attract.

## Keep your listings consistent

Incorrect room descriptions, outdated photographs, missing amenities, and inconsistent policies can create confusion before a guest even arrives.

Review your listings regularly. Make sure room details, cancellation policies, property facilities, and photographs accurately represent the experience you provide.

Consistency also matters when availability and rates change. Poor coordination can lead to avoidable booking problems and additional work for your team.

## Look beyond booking volume

A channel that generates many reservations is not necessarily your strongest channel financially.

Consider the commission, the rate offered, the length of stay, cancellation behaviour, and the operational effort associated with each booking source.

The aim is not to eliminate online travel agencies. It is to understand their role in your overall distribution strategy.

## Make distribution an ongoing process

Demand changes with seasons, local events, competitor activity, and traveller behaviour. A distribution plan should be reviewed regularly rather than set once and forgotten.

Monitor performance, update listings, review promotions, and adjust your channel mix when the evidence supports a change.

## The takeaway

A strong OTA strategy combines visibility with control. The right channels, accurate listings, coordinated availability, and regular performance reviews can help hotels make better decisions about how they reach potential guests.
